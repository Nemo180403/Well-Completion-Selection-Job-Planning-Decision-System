## *Now further if you want to isolate the perforated zone we use bridge plug. Now Bridge plug we use come with different types (depending on setting procedure and duration of isolation).
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Bridge Plugs] --> B{Isolation Duration}
    B -->|"6 months to 1 year<br/>(e.g. Testing Phase)"| C[Retrievable Bridge Plug]
    B -->|More than 1 year| D[Permanent Bridge Plug]
    C --> E[Hydraulic Setting, using ball and pressurizing through tubing]
    C --> F[Mechanical Setting, through wireline]
    D --> G[Hydraulic Setting, using tubing and hydraulic setting tool]
    D --> H[Mechanical Setting, through wireline and tubing by rotation]
```
